This is a plain-text backup of the fields in `publish/index.html`.

## Description

Sapling is text version control: it records how your writing changes over time, lets you jump back to earlier states, and creates branches when you edit from the past.

- Write in the editor and Sapling records each change.
- Click the timeline to navigate through versions.
- Edit from an earlier point to create a new branch.
- Export/import history as JSON to share or back up your work.

Privacy: no account, no analytics, no data sent to the developer. Your history is stored locally in the browser.
